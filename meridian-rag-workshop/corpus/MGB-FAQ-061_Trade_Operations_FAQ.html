Trade Operations FAQ

Document ID: MGB-FAQ-061 | Version 2025.1 | Effective 2025-05-01 | Owner: Global Trade Operations | Jurisdiction: GLOBAL

How quickly do we check documents under a letter of credit?

Our target is three banking days from presentation.

Can a client ask us to ignore a discrepancy?

The applicant can consent in writing to waive discrepancies. Who approves the waiver depends on the amount. See the waiver approval matrix.

Who do I call about a vessel I'm unsure of?

Escalate to Sanctions before processing the transaction.

What is the cut-off for same-day letter of credit issuance?

Check the trade desk quick reference card for local cut-off times.

How long can a usance letter of credit run?

Up to 360 days without extra approval.
