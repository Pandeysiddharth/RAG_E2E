Documentary Collections Procedure

Document ID: MGB-TF-PROC-035 | Version 2025.1 | Effective 2025-02-01 | Owner: Global Trade Operations | Jurisdiction: GLOBAL

1. Purpose

This procedure covers export and import documentary collections handled by MGB.

2. Types of collection

Under documents against payment (D/P), documents are released only when the drawee pays. Under documents against acceptance (D/A), documents are released when the drawee accepts a bill of exchange.

3. Release and follow-up

Documents may be released against acceptance only on the client's written instruction. While a collection remains unpaid, a tracer must be sent to the collecting bank every 30 days.
