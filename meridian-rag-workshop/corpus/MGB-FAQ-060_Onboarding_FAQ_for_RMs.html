Onboarding FAQ for Relationship Managers

Document ID: MGB-FAQ-060 | Version 2024.2 | Effective 2024-06-10 | Owner: CIB Onboarding Operations | Jurisdiction: GLOBAL

How long does onboarding take?

Most low and medium risk clients are onboarded within two weeks once we have everything we need. High risk clients take about four weeks.

How often will my client be reviewed?

High risk clients are reviewed every year. Medium risk clients are reviewed every three years, and low risk clients every five years.

My client is listed on a stock exchange. Do we still need ownership details?

If the exchange is on our approved list, we only need evidence of the listing.

Can I start a transaction before onboarding is finished?

No. No account can be opened and no transaction processed until onboarding is approved.

What happens if my client's documents are old?

Proof of address must be recent, and identity documents must not have expired. Ask the client for new copies.
