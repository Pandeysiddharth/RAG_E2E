UK KYC Addendum

Document ID: MGB-KYC-ADD-UK | Version 2025.1 | Effective 2025-03-01 | Owner: UK Financial Crime Compliance | Jurisdiction: UK

1. Application

This addendum applies to all CIB clients booked in the United Kingdom. It supplements the Global Customer Due Diligence Policy (MGB-KYC-POL-001). Where this addendum is silent, the global policy applies.

2. Persons with significant control (PSC)

For UK-incorporated clients, Onboarding Operations must check the Companies House register of persons with significant control (PSC) and compare it with the ownership information provided by the client.

Any discrepancy between the PSC register and the client-provided ownership must be reported to Onboarding Compliance within 5 business days of identification. The case may not be approved until Onboarding Compliance has recorded its assessment of the discrepancy.

3. Periodic review

UK clients follow the global periodic review cycles. No UK-specific cycle applies.
